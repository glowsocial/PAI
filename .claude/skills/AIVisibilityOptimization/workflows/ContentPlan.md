# Content Plan Workflow

**Trigger:** "content plan for [domain]", "what should [business] publish", "AI-optimized content strategy"

## Purpose

Create a content strategy optimized for AI visibility - focusing on the formats and topics AI systems cite most.

## Key Insight

AI systems cite certain content types disproportionately:
- **Listicles:** 32% of citations
- **Q&A/FAQ:** 24% of citations
- **How-To:** 18% of citations
- **Comparisons:** 14% of citations

The strategy: Create content in these formats for high-intent queries.

## Process

### Step 1: Identify Core Topics

Based on the business, identify:
- Main service/product categories (3-5)
- Common customer questions (10-20)
- Competitor/alternative comparisons (3-5)
- Process/how-to topics (5-10)

### Step 2: Map to Content Formats

| Topic Type | Best Format | Example |
|------------|-------------|---------|
| "What is X?" | Q&A with FAQ schema | "What is AI automation consulting?" |
| "Best X for Y" | Listicle | "5 Best Automation Tools for Mid-Market" |
| "X vs Y" | Comparison | "n8n vs Make.com: Which Is Right for You?" |
| "How to X" | How-To guide | "How to Calculate Automation ROI" |
| "X cost/pricing" | Q&A/Guide | "How Much Does Automation Consulting Cost?" |

### Step 3: Create Content Calendar

**Month 1: Foundation (12 pieces)**
- 4 Q&A posts (core questions)
- 4 FAQ-style pages (service pages)
- 2 How-To guides
- 2 Comparison posts

**Month 2: Authority (12 pieces)**
- 4 Q&A posts
- 2 Case studies
- 2 Listicles ("Top 5...", "Best...")
- 2 How-To guides
- 2 Industry-specific content

**Month 3: Scale (16 pieces)**
- 8 Q&A posts
- 4 Comparison posts
- 2 Listicles
- 2 Guest posts (for backlinks)

### Step 4: Content Specifications

**For AI Extractability:**

1. **Answer Block** (40-60 words)
   - Put at the TOP of the article
   - Direct answer to the title question
   - This is what AI extracts

2. **Structure**
   - Use H2s and H3s clearly
   - Bullet points and numbered lists
   - Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)

3. **Length**
   - Q&A posts: 300-500 words
   - How-To guides: 800-1500 words
   - Comparisons: 1000-2000 words

4. **Schema**
   - Every Q&A page gets FAQPage schema
   - How-To pages get HowTo schema
   - Add Article schema to blog posts

```markdown

**[40-60 word answer block that directly answers the question. Include your business name. This text is what AI systems extract.]**

## The Detailed Answer

[Expanded explanation, 200-300 words]

## Key Takeaways

- [Point 1]
- [Point 2]
- [Point 3]

## Related Questions

- [Link to related Q&A 1]
- [Link to related Q&A 2]
```

## Schema to Add

Each content piece should include:
- Article schema (all posts)
- FAQPage schema (Q&A posts)
- HowTo schema (how-to guides)

## Success Metrics

Track these after 90 days:
- Indexed pages (should 3-4x)
- AI mention rate (retest queries)
- Organic traffic from AI-referred sources
