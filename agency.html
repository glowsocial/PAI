Questions?

What happens when I hit my client limit?
You'll see a prompt to upgrade to the next tier. Your existing clients keep working—nothing breaks. You just can't add new sub-accounts until you upgrade.
Can my clients log in to their own accounts?
Not yet—right now it's a master login with sub-accounts you control. Client logins are on our roadmap if there's demand.
What if I need to remove a client?
You can archive or delete sub-accounts anytime. If you drop below a tier threshold, you can downgrade at your next billing cycle.
Is there a contract or commitment?
No contracts. Cancel anytime. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you in.
Do you offer white-labeling?
Not currently, but it's something we're considering for larger agencies. Reach out if it's important to you—your feedback shapes our roadmap.
