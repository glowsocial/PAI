### Series Design Notes

- **Typography:** Bold, clean, sans-serif. The word should hit hard.
- **Size:** 3" x 1.5" rectangle or 3" circle (test both)
- **Colors:** Consider a cohesive palette across the series, or let each have its own color
- **Branding:** No Glow Social logo on front. Optional small "glowsocial.io" on back or edge.
- **Material:** Weatherproof vinyl if they'll go on doors/windows
- **Die-cut option:** Custom shapes (badge, shield, banner) can elevate the look

### Print Quantity Suggestion

For a first run at networking events:
- 50 of each phrase = 300 total stickers
- Or weight toward "Fiercely Local" and "Proudly Local" (most universal)

### Conversation Starter

When someone picks one: *"Which one speaks to you?"*

This opens a conversation about their business, their values, and why they do what they do—which leads naturally into what you do.

## Stock Recommendations (Clubcard)

### Postcards: **18pt Coated One Side (C1S)** ← Recommended

| Stock | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|-------|------|------|----------|
| **18pt C1S** | Glossy front for vibrant colors, uncoated back for writing, substantial weight | Standard white look | Your main postcard order |
| **16pt Bamboo** | 90% bamboo/10% cotton, eco-story, unique texture, stands out | Colors less vibrant, texture may compete with design | If you want "small business solidarity" vibe |
| **18pt Natural Kraft** | Rustic, artisanal, very distinctive | Dark background limits design options, harder to read | Not ideal for your postcards |
| **15pt Recycled** | 100% recycled, eco-friendly messaging | Standard white, less distinctive | Good middle ground if eco matters |

**My recommendation:** Go **18pt C1S** for postcards. You're positioning Glow Social as a marketing agency—it should look polished. The $49 price does the "accessible" work; the design can feel professional. Plus the uncoated back means people can write notes on it.

**If you want to experiment:** Order a small batch of **16pt Bamboo** alongside your main C1S order. The bamboo texture could be great for local business events where you want the "we're in this together" feel. Test both.

### Business Cards

**16pt Bamboo** or **18pt Hemp** — This is where textured/eco stocks shine. Business cards are personal, and the tactile experience matters. A bamboo card reinforces "local, thoughtful, small business."

### Stickers (StickerGiant)

**Weatherproof vinyl** — For the "[Adverb] Local" series. These might go on doors, windows, laptops. Vinyl holds up.

## Bamboo/Kraft vs White: When to Use Each

| Material | Use When... | Skip When... |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| **White (coated)** | You need colors to pop, photos to look sharp, professional/polished feel | You want to stand out from typical marketing materials |
| **Bamboo/Hemp** | Identity pieces, business cards, "small business solidarity" messaging | You have detailed photography or need precise color matching |
| **Kraft** | Very intentional rustic/artisan brand, bold simple graphics only | Your design has light colors or detailed elements (they'll get lost) |

**The move:** White C1S postcards (your main leave-behind), bamboo business cards (tactile/memorable), vinyl stickers (durable).
