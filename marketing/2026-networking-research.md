## BNI (Business Network International)

### Overview
- 85+ chapters across Metro Phoenix, Prescott, and Flagstaff
- Structured referral-passing organization
- One member per business category (exclusive)
- Weekly morning meetings required

### Cost
- **Application Fee:** Varies by region
- **Annual Membership:** $899/year (BNI Arizona) or $625/year (BNI Arizona South)
- **Two-Year Option:** $1,599 (Arizona) or $950 (Arizona South)
- Monthly payment plans available through PayPal

### Chandler/East Valley Chapters
- **BNI Foothills Sharks** - Wednesdays 8am
- **BNI Apex** - Thursdays 9am
- **BNI Latino** - Thursdays 9:30am

### Pros
- Structured referral system with tracking
- Exclusive category lock (no competing members)
- High accountability and engagement

### Cons
- Weekly attendance required (miss 3+, lose seat)
- Highest cost option
- Must pass referrals or risk membership
- Morning meetings (7-8:30am typical)

### Contact
- Website: [bniarizona.com](https://bniarizona.com)
- Phone: 602-788-1606

## Chambers of Commerce

### Chandler Chamber of Commerce

**Membership:**
- Entrepreneur Level: $410/year
- Higher tiers available for larger businesses

**Networking Events:**
- "Wake Up Chandler" - Morning networking (monthly)
- "Business After Business" - Evening networking (monthly)
- Ribbon cuttings, mixers, workshops

**Website:** [chandlerchamber.com](https://chandlerchamber.com)

---

### Gilbert Chamber of Commerce

**Overview:**
- 620+ members, established 1978
- Strong local business community

**Membership Tiers:**
1. **Entrepreneur** - For solopreneurs/businesses under 3 years
2. **Business** - Expanded listing with photos, keywords
3. **Champion** - Includes US Chamber membership
4. **Executive** - Conference room access, referral introductions
5. **President's Circle** - Top tier with maximum visibility

**Networking Events:**
- **Coffee & Connect** - Monthly Friday mornings at member businesses
- **Chamber Connections** - Relaxed networking with food
- **Gilbert YP** - For ages 21-40
- **Nine & Networking** - Golf networking events
- **Women Empowered Committee** - Women-focused socials
- **Executive Roundtable** - For businesses with 15+ employees
- **Veterans in Business Council** - Veteran entrepreneurs

**Standout Feature:** Free for members, $20 for non-members at many events - good for testing before committing.

**Contact:**
- Website: [gilbertaz.com](https://gilbertaz.com)
- Business Development: Antoinette@gilbertchamber.com
- Address: 119 N Gilbert Rd, Gilbert, AZ 85234

## Recommended Strategy for 2026

### Phase 1: Low-Commitment Testing (Q1)
1. Attend one Gilbert Chamber "Coffee & Connect" as non-member ($20)
2. Visit 2 BNI chapters as a guest (free)
3. Attend one Business Women of East Valley meeting
4. Try one Meetup group event

### Phase 2: Commit to 1-2 Organizations (Q2)
Based on Phase 1 results, join:
- **If referrals are priority:** BNI (~$900/year)
- **If local visibility is priority:** Gilbert or Chandler Chamber (~$400-500/year)
- **If women-focused is priority:** NAWBO or Business Women of East Valley

### Phase 3: Maximize Value (Q3-Q4)
- Volunteer for committee roles
- Sponsor one event
- Host one event at your location (if applicable)
- Collect testimonials from networking connections

## Questions to Ask When Visiting

1. "What industries are most represented here?"
2. "How do members typically share referrals?"
3. "What's the attendance requirement?"
4. "Can I see examples of businesses like mine that have succeeded here?"
5. "What's the time commitment beyond meetings?"

## Notes

- Most organizations allow 1-2 guest visits before joining
- Evening options exist (Business Women of East Valley, some Meetups)
