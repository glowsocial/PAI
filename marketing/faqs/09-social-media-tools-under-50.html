Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best social media management tool under $50 a month?

It depends on whether you want to create content yourself or have it done for you. For DIY scheduling, Buffer offers the best balance of simplicity and features starting at $6/month per channel. For done-for-you posting where content is created and published automatically, Glow Social ($49/month) is the only option under $50 that includes content creation.

Are free social media tools good enough for a small business?

Free tools like Meta Business Suite and Buffer's free tier work fine for scheduling — but they don't create content for you. The real cost is your time: 3–5 hours per month writing captions, designing images, and planning what to post. If that time is worth more than $15/hour, a $49/month done-for-you service is actually cheaper.

What's the difference between a scheduling tool and a done-for-you service?

A scheduling tool (Buffer, Later, Hootsuite) lets you schedule posts you've already created — you still write captions, design images, and decide what to post. A done-for-you service like Glow Social creates the content for you and publishes it automatically. Scheduling tools save you from posting manually every day; done-for-you services save you from creating content at all.

Getting Started
