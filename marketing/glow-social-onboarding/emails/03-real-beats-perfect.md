# Email 3: The "Good Enough" Philosophy

**Subject:** Permission to be unpolished
**Preview:** Your customers don't want a Super Bowl ad.

---

Here’s a secret about local customers:

They don’t trust perfect.

They trust *real*.

They want to see the owner (you). They want to see the team. They want to see the "messy" behind-the-scenes reality of a local business.

That’s your superpower. The big chains *can’t* be real. They have to pass everything through legal.

You can just snap a photo and share it.

Glow Social is here to help you polish the caption and schedule it, but the magic? The magic is your business.

Don't hide it.

Kathleen
