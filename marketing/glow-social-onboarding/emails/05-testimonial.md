# Email 5: The Founder Ask

**Subject:** I have a favor to ask
**Preview:** We're the new kids on the block.

---

I’ll be honest: Glow Social is new.

We aren't a giant Silicon Valley corporation. We're a small team building this because we saw local businesses getting left behind.

Because we're new, we don't have a massive marketing budget. We rely on people like you.

If you’ve found Glow Social helpful so far, would you be willing to share a quick sentence about your experience?

**You can just hit reply.**

Tell me what you like, what you've posted, or just "It saves me time."

(And if you *haven't* loved it? Please tell me that too. I read every email to fix the things that aren't working.)

Thank you for betting on us.

Kathleen
