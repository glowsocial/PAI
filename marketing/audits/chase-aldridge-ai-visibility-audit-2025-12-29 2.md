## Top 5 Quick Wins

### 1. Get Listed on Clutch.co + GoodFirms (2-3 hours)

**Impact:** +7-10 points
**Why:** These are heavily cited by AI for consultant recommendations. Free to list.

**Action Steps:**
1. Go to [clutch.co/register](https://clutch.co/register) and create a company profile
2. Go to [goodfirms.co/add-your-company](https://www.goodfirms.co/add-your-company) and submit
3. Fill out all fields completely - AI systems favor complete profiles
