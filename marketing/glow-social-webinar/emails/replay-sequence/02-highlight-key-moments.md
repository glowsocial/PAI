### Takeaway #1: The Visibility Paradox

**The Problem:**
You're not losing customers because your product/service is worse. You're losing because you're INVISIBLE online.

- 70% of customers check social media before visiting a local business

**The Solution:**
You don't need better content. You need MORE CONSISTENT content.

Posting 3x/week with simple content beats posting 1x/month with "perfect" content.

**Why it matters:**
Corporate chains have teams posting daily. You can't compete on time. But you CAN compete on consistency with the right system.
