## Email Body

[First Name],

Sarah Mitchell owns "The Daily Grind," a local coffee shop in Portland.

Her coffee is incredible. Her regulars love her. Her reviews are 4.8 stars.

But she was losing walk-in customers to Starbucks.

Not because Starbucks has better coffee (they don't).

Because Starbucks *looked* more active online.

### The Moment That Changed Everything

Last summer, one of Sarah's regular customers walked in and said:

**"I thought you closed down - your Instagram looked dead. I almost went to Starbucks instead."**

That hit Sarah hard.

Her last Instagram post? Six months old. "Happy Thanksgiving 2024\!" in January.

Her Facebook page? Gathering digital dust.

She wasn't lazy. She wasn't bad at business. She was just... **busy actually running the business.**

### The $2,400 Mistake

Sarah tried hiring twice:
- **First hire:** Quit after 3 months without notice
- **Second hire:** Posted generic content that didn't fit her brand

Total wasted: $2,400
Total posts that actually represented her business: Maybe 5

She was about to give up on social media entirely.

### What Changed

Sarah found the Glow Social founding member offer through a Facebook ad.

She was skeptical. "$25/month? Why so cheap? What's the catch?"

But the free first month meant no risk. So she tried it.

**The setup:** 5 minutes. She answered questions about her business, connected Instagram and Facebook, done.

**Week 1:** First batch of 12 posts generated. She reviewed them, made a few tweaks, scheduled.

**Week 2:** Posts started going live automatically. Customers were commenting.

**Month 1:** New followers asking about menu items they saw in posts.

**Month 3:** That same regular customer told her: *"Your Instagram looks so professional now. What changed?"*
