### I'm Not a Social Media Expert

I don't have a huge following.

I don't love creating content.

Honestly? **I find social media exhausting.**

If you'd told me five years ago I'd be building a social media company, I would have laughed.

But here's what I kept seeing:

---

### Talented Businesses Losing to Mediocre Competitors

The family restaurant with incredible food was losing to Applebee's.

The local gym with amazing trainers was losing to Planet Fitness.

The boutique salon with expert stylists was losing to Great Clips.

Not because they were worse.

**Because they were invisible online.**

Their last Instagram post? Six months old. Their Facebook? Gathering dust.

Meanwhile, the corporate chains had full social media teams posting daily.

And customers scrolled past the dead pages and thought: *"Are they even still open?"*

---

### The Problem With "Just Post More"

Every social media guru says the same thing:

*"Post consistently\! Be authentic\! Engage with your audience\!"*

Cool. And when exactly am I supposed to do that between running payroll, managing inventory, handling customer complaints, and actually doing the work that pays the bills?

The advice is technically correct. But it's useless for people who are actually busy.

---

### The Problem With Hiring

So businesses try hiring.

And one of three things happens:

1. **They quit** (usually without notice, right when you need them most)
2. **They're mediocre** (generic posts that don't fit your brand)
3. **They're expensive** ($300-500/month, plus training, plus management overhead)

I watched friends waste thousands on social media managers who either disappeared or posted garbage.

---

### The Realization

You shouldn't need to be good at social media to stay competitive.

You should just need to **show up.**

Not as an influencer. Not as a content creator. Just as a business that's active, professional, and visible.

That's it.

---

### So I Built Glow Social

Not because I love social media.

But because I hated watching talented business owners lose customers to competitors who were just... more visible.

**The goal was simple:**

Make it possible for busy business owners to look as active and professional as corporate chains with full social media teams - without the work, the hiring headaches, or the thousands of dollars.

Just the outputs. No fluff.

---

### Why I'm Launching With 50 Founding Members

I'm not scaling this to thousands of users immediately.

I want to prove this works with 50 real businesses first.

Restaurants. Salons. Gyms. Professional services. Retail stores.

If this solves the problem for 50 founding members, then I know it works. And then we scale.

**That's why the founding rate is $25/month instead of $497/month.**

You're not buying a finished product. You're helping build the thing that levels the playing field for local businesses.

And in exchange, you lock in $25/month for life.
