**Why it fails:**
- Generic (could be any business)

- Sounds like a robot trying to be motivational

### Good AI Content (What Glow Social Actually Does)

**Example Post:**
"Quick PSA for homeowners: If your roof is more than 15 years old and you're seeing granules in your gutters, that's not normal wear and tear. That's your roof telling you it's failing. DM us for a free inspection before the next storm hits."

**Why it works:**
- Specific to the business (roofing)
- Educational (provides value)
- Conversational (sounds human)
- Clear call to action

### What Makes the Difference?

**1. Industry-Specific Training**
We don't just prompt AI to "write a post."

We use the 196 proven post ideas framework - content templates that have worked for successful businesses in YOUR specific industry.

A restaurant gets different content than a salon. A gym gets different content than a professional service.

**2. Business Context**
The AI knows:
- Your business name
- What you do
- Your location (if local)
- Your target audience
- Your services/products

So the content is personalized to YOU, not generic.

**3. You Have Full Control**
Before anything goes live, you review it.

Don't like a post? Edit it. Regenerate it. Rewrite it completely.

The AI drafts. You approve. Nothing publishes without your say-so.

Think of it like hiring a writer who drafts everything, then you give final approval.

**Generic AI Post (What You're Worried About):**

One sounds like a robot. One sounds like a knowledgeable barista.
