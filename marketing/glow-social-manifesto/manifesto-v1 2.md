# Glow Social Manifesto (Draft v1)

---

## We Exist for the Locals

The coffee shop owner who knows your order by heart.

The gym where the trainer remembers your kid's name.

The salon that's been on Main Street for 30 years.

The restaurant where the owner is also the one refilling your water.

**These are the businesses that make a town worth living in.**

They don't have corporate budgets. They don't have marketing teams. They don't have investors demanding growth at all costs.

They have rent. They have payroll. They have customers who became friends.

And they're getting crushed online by businesses that don't know their names.

---

## The Visibility War Is Rigged

Here's what's happening:

Private equity is buying up local businesses and turning them into chains.

Out-of-state corporations are opening locations in your town with full marketing teams on day one.

Algorithms reward whoever posts the most - which means whoever has the most resources wins.

The local bakery that's been there since 1987 looks dead online.

The new chain that opened last month looks thriving.

Not because the chain is better. Because they have people whose entire job is to post.

**The game is rigged against the people who are actually doing the work.**

---

## We Think That's Wrong

A business shouldn't lose customers because the owner was too busy *actually running the business* to post on Instagram.

A family restaurant shouldn't look less professional than Applebee's just because they can't afford a social media manager.

A local gym shouldn't hemorrhage members to Planet Fitness because Planet Fitness posted yesterday and they didn't.

**Visibility shouldn't be a luxury only corporations can afford.**

---

## We're Here to Level the Playing Field

Glow Social exists for one reason:

**To make local businesses look as active and professional online as the chains - without the work, the headache, or the corporate budget.**

Not so they can become influencers.

Not so they can go viral.

Just so they can show up. Consistently. Like the chains do.

So when someone new moves to town and searches "best coffee near me," the local shop looks just as alive as Starbucks.

So the family business doesn't lose to the corporation that just showed up more.

---

## Who We're For

We're for the businesses that:

- Live in the communities they serve
- Know their customers by name
- Care about more than just profit
- Don't have time to figure out social media
- Refuse to let the chains win

We're for the locals.

---

## Who We're Not For

We're not for private equity.

We're not for corporations pretending to be local.

We're not for anyone trying to extract value from a community they don't live in.

If your business model depends on squeezing out the little guy, we're not your tool.

---

## The Mission

**Keep Main Street visible.**

That's it.

We want local businesses to stay in business. And in 2024, that means being visible online.

We're not trying to turn business owners into content creators.

We're trying to keep them from being invisible.

---

## This Is Personal

We built Glow Social because we're tired of watching good businesses lose to mediocre chains.

We're tired of seeing "For Lease" signs where family businesses used to be.

We're tired of every town looking the same - the same chains, the same signs, the same soulless storefronts.

**Local businesses are what make a place worth living in.**

And we're going to do everything we can to help them survive.

---

*Glow Social. For the locals.*
