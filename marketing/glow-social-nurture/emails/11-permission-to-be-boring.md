

## Permission to be boring on social media

	Nobody expects you to dance on Reels.

I want to give you permission to be boring on social media.

Seriously.

You don't have to:
- Go viral
- Be funny
- Dance on Reels
- Share hot takes
- "Build a personal brand"
- Hop on trending audios

You're a local business. Not a content creator.

The pressure you feel? It's fake.

It comes from watching influencers and thinking that's what social media is supposed to look like.

But those people are in a completely different game. Their product IS the content. Your content is just a signal that your actual business exists.

Completely different games.

Here's what your customers actually want to see:

- That you're open
- What you offer
- That real humans work there
- Occasional reminders that you exist

That's it.

They're not following you for entertainment. They're following you because they went to your business once and might want to go again.

So post the "boring" stuff:
- Photo of your shop
- Picture of your product
- Your team doing their thing
- A simple "Happy Friday from [Business]"

Nobody will complain it's not creative enough. Because nobody expected you to be creative in the first place.

You don't need to compete with influencers. You need to compete with Starbucks.

And Starbucks isn't creative either. They're just consistent.

The bar is so much lower than you think. You just have to clear it.
