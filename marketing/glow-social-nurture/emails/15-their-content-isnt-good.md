# Week 15: Their Content Isn't That Good

**Subject:** Look closer at Starbucks' posts

**Preview:** It's not the quality. It's the quantity.

Go look at your nearest chain's social media.

Seriously. Go look. I'll wait.

Notice anything?

It's... not that good.

Generic photos. Basic captions. Nothing clever. Nothing viral. Nothing you couldn't do yourself.

"Happy Monday! Start your week with [product]."

"It's finally [season]! Come try our [seasonal thing]."

"Thanks for being a loyal customer!"

That's it. That's the content that's beating you.

Not genius creative. Not professional photography. Not viral trends.

Just... showing up. Regularly. With basic stuff.

The chains don't win because they're better at content. They win because they never stop posting.

You're overthinking this.

You're waiting to post something good when good enough is all you need.

The bar they're clearing isn't high. It's just consistent.

You don't need to be more creative than Starbucks. You just need to be as consistent.

That's the whole secret. They're not doing anything special. They're just not stopping.
