# Week 24: Stop Comparing Yourself to Influencers

**Subject:** You're playing the wrong game

**Preview:** They're entertainers. You're a business.

You scroll Instagram and see:

Perfectly edited Reels. Trending audio. Dancing. Pointing at text. Flawless lighting.

And you think: "I can't compete with that."

You're right. You can't.

But here's the thing: you're not supposed to.

Those are influencers. Content creators. People whose entire business IS the content.

Their product is attention. They need millions of views to make money.

Your product is whatever you actually sell. You need customers in your town to make money.

Completely different games.

An influencer needs to go viral. You need to look alive.

An influencer needs millions of followers. You need the hundred people in your community who might come in.

An influencer needs to entertain. You need to exist.

The mistake is comparing yourself to the wrong benchmark.

You're not competing with influencers. You're competing with Starbucks.

And Starbucks isn't making Reels either. They're just posting consistently.

Stop feeling bad that you can't dance on camera. You don't need to.

You just need to show up. Regularly. With basic stuff.
