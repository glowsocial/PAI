# Week 28: There's No Perfect Time to Post

**Subject:** Stop waiting for the "right moment"

**Preview:** Tuesday at 2pm isn't magic.

"When's the best time to post?"

I get this question constantly. Like there's some secret optimal moment when the algorithm gods smile upon you.

Tuesday at 2pm. Wednesday at 10am. Sunday evening.

People obsess over this.

Here's the truth: for local businesses, it barely matters.

The best time to post is when you actually post.

The "optimal posting time" advice is for influencers trying to maximize viral reach. They need every edge.

You? You need to look alive.

A post at 11pm on a Saturday is infinitely better than no post at all.

The person searching for "hair salon near me" tomorrow doesn't care that you posted at a suboptimal time. They care that you posted recently.

The referral checking out your page doesn't think "this post was at 3am, how unprofessional." They think "okay, they posted this week, looks active."

Waiting for the perfect time is another form of not posting.

The chains don't wait. They have content scheduled around the clock because any time is better than no time.

Stop optimizing. Start posting.

[Glow Social](https://glowsocial.com) helps you just get posts out there. Timing is a detail for later.
