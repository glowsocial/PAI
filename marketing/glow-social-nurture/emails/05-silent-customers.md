# Week 5: The Customers You Never Hear From

**Subject:** "I thought you guys closed down"

**Preview:** The feedback business owners never hear.

A friend of mine is a financial advisor.

Great reviews. Loyal clients. Been in business 8 years.

Last year, a longtime client mentioned during a meeting:

"Oh good, you're still taking new clients! I almost referred my sister to someone else because your LinkedIn looked inactive - I wasn't sure you were still growing your practice."

She hadn't posted in 4 months. Not because business was bad - because business was *good*. She was busy actually serving clients.

But to someone checking online? It looked like she might have closed.

Here's the thing:

Customers don't tell you when they *don't* come in.

You never hear "I was going to hire you but your social media looked abandoned, so I went with someone else."

They just... go somewhere else. Quietly. To the chain that looks active.

The only reason my friend found out was because that client happened to mention it.

How many didn't?

Every dead social page is leaking customers. Slowly. Invisibly.

Not because of anything wrong with the business. Just because of the perception created by silence.

People check. They see nothing recent. They move on to whoever looks alive.

Your regulars know you're open. But new customers? People passing through? People who just moved to town?

They're checking online first. And they're making judgments in seconds.

The chain down the street posted yesterday. Did you?
