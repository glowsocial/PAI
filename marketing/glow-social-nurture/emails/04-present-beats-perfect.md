# Week 4: Present Beats Perfect

**Subject:** Your competitor's ugly posts are beating you

**Preview:** Done beats perfect. Every time.

I talked to an attorney last month who hadn't posted in 6 months.

She had ideas. Good ones, actually. Useful tips about estate planning that her clients always asked about.

But she never posted them because:

"I need to make sure it's legally accurate for every state."
"I should have someone review it first."
"I want to wait until I can do a whole series on this."
"I need to write something that sounds professional."

Meanwhile, LegalZoom posted a generic tip with a stock photo and the caption "Planning ahead matters."

Here's the unfair part:

LegalZoom's mediocre post says "we're here, we're helpful, we're active."

The thoughtful content sitting in her head says nothing. Because nobody sees it.

Your customers aren't judging your photography. They're not analyzing your caption. They're not comparing your grid to influencers.

They're making a split-second judgment: active or abandoned?

The businesses that look alive online aren't the ones with the best content.

They're the ones who actually post.

Present beats perfect. Every time.

If you've got ideas sitting in your head waiting to be "polished enough" - just post one today. Any one. With whatever words come to mind.

It'll do more for your business than the perfect post you're still planning.
