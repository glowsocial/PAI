# Week 19: The Algorithm Myth

**Subject:** Stop blaming the algorithm

**Preview:** It's not suppressing you. You're just not posting.

"The algorithm is killing my reach."

I hear this constantly. And look, I get it. It feels like Instagram and Facebook are actively working against small businesses.

But here's the uncomfortable truth:

For most local businesses, the algorithm isn't the problem.

The problem is you're not posting enough for the algorithm to even matter.

The algorithm affects reach. How many of your followers see your posts.

But if you're posting once a month? Twice a month? The algorithm isn't your bottleneck.

**Not posting** is your bottleneck.

The algorithm is a problem for influencers trying to reach millions. For creators whose business model depends on viral reach.

For a local business? You don't need viral reach. You need to exist.

You need someone searching for "dentist near me" to land on a page that looks active.

You need referrals to find a page that confirms what their friend said.

You need new residents to see recent posts and think "okay, they're legit."

None of that requires beating the algorithm. It requires showing up.

The chains don't worry about the algorithm. They just post. Every day. Forever.

Stop blaming the algorithm. Start posting.
