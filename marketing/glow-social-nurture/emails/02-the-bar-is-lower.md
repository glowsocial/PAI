# Week 2: The Bar Is Lower Than You Think

**Subject:** You don't need to be an influencer

**Preview:** Nobody's checking if you posted on Tuesday.

Every social media expert says the same thing:

"Post consistently! Be authentic! Engage with your audience!"

And then they show you their content calendar with 47 slots per week.

Here's the problem:

That advice is for influencers. For content creators. For people whose *job* is social media.

Not for people who live where they work and are too busy serving customers to post about it.

Let me tell you what "showing up" actually looks like for a local business:

**Not this:** Posting 3x daily, engaging for 2 hours, creating Reels, doing Stories, going Live

**This:** Posting 2-3 times per week so your page doesn't look abandoned

That's it.

Nobody is checking if you posted on Tuesday. No customer is thinking "well, they didn't post yesterday, so I'll go to their competitor."

They're just looking at your page and making a split-second judgment: active or closed?

The bar is so much lower than you think.

You don't need to go viral. You don't need to dance on Reels. You don't need to "build a personal brand."

You just need to look alive.

12 posts a month. That's 3 per week. That's enough to not lose customers to the chain down the street.
