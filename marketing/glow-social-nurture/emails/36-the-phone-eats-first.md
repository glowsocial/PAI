# Week 36: The Phone Eats First

**Subject:** The phrase that changed everything

**Preview:** Make it a habit, not a decision.

"The phone eats first."

It's a saying in food photography. Before you take a bite, you take a photo.

It's become such a habit that people do it automatically. No decision. No willpower. Just: food arrives, phone comes out.

What if you had the same habit for your business?

Customer leaves happy? Phone comes out.
Finished a project? Phone comes out.
Shop looks good today? Phone comes out.

Not every photo gets posted. But if you're always capturing, you always have content.

Right now, you're trying to remember to take photos when you're busy doing actual work.

That doesn't work. You're focused on the customer, the job, the service.

The trick is making it automatic. A habit, not a decision.

Some people put a sign in their workspace: "Did you take a photo?"

Some set a daily phone alarm: "Content moment."

Some make it the last step of every job: work is done when the photo is taken.

Find your trigger. Make it automatic.

The chains have systems. You need habits.
