# Week 18: The New Resident

**Subject:** 40 million people moved last year

**Preview:** They're all searching for you. What do they find?

40 million Americans moved to a new home last year.

That's 40 million people who needed to find a new dentist. A new hair salon. A new mechanic. A new everything.

How do they find these businesses?

They search.

"Best pizza near me"
"Hair salon [neighborhood]"
"Plumber in [city]"

And then they look at what comes up.

The chains show up. Of course. They've optimized for this. Active pages. Recent reviews. Posts from yesterday.

Do you show up? And if you do, what do they see?

Here's the thing about new residents:

They have no loyalty yet. They don't know anyone to ask. They're making decisions based entirely on what they can see online.

Your regulars know you're great. They'll drive past three competitors to get to you.

But new residents? They're picking whoever looks alive.

Every person who moves to your town is a potential customer up for grabs. The chains know this. That's why they're everywhere, all the time.

You're not competing for your existing customers. They already chose you.

You're competing for the 40 million people who moved last year and are still deciding.
