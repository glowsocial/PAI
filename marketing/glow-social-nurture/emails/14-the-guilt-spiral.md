# Week 14: The Guilt Spiral

**Subject:** The worst part about not posting

**Preview:** It's not the missed customers. It's the feeling.

The worst part about not posting isn't the business you lose.

It's the guilt.

You know you should post. You think about it constantly. Every time you open Instagram, there's that little voice: "You should post something. When's the last time you posted? Why can't you just do this one simple thing?"

So you avoid opening the app entirely. Which makes it worse.

Now there's a gap. And the gap makes you feel like you need to come back with something *good*. Something that justifies the silence.

But you're busy. And tired. And nothing feels good enough.

So you don't post. And the gap grows. And the guilt compounds.

This is the spiral:

Don't post → Feel guilty → Avoid the app → Gap grows → Need something "good" to break the silence → Nothing feels good enough → Don't post → Feel more guilty

Meanwhile, the chains don't have this problem. They have systems. They don't rely on motivation or guilt. The machine just runs.

You're not lazy. You're not bad at social media. You're just stuck in a spiral that feeds itself.

The only way out is to break the cycle. Post something. Anything. A photo of your coffee. Your storefront. Literally anything.

Or let [Glow Social](https://glowsocial.com) break it for you.

Either way - stop letting the guilt win.
