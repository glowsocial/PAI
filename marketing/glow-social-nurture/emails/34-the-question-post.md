# Week 34: Ask a Question

**Subject:** The post that writes itself

**Preview:** Let your audience do the work.

Stuck on what to post?

Ask a question.

"Coffee or tea? ☕️"
"What's your go-to [thing related to your business]?"
"Poll: Do you prefer [option A] or [option B]?"
"What's the best [thing] you've had this week?"

Questions are magic because:

1. They're incredibly easy to create
2. They don't require photos or graphics
3. They get engagement (people love sharing opinions)
4. They make your page look active

A question post takes 30 seconds to write.

And when people comment? Your page suddenly looks alive with conversation.

The chains use questions constantly. "What's your Monday coffee order?" "Pick your favorite fall drink!" "This or that?"

They know it works.

You don't need clever content. You need conversation.

A simple question creates conversation with almost no effort.

Use questions when you're stuck. Use them when you're tired. Use them when you have nothing else.

They always work.
