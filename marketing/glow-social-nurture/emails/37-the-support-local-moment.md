# Week 37: "Support Local" Is Your Moment

**Subject:** Ride the wave while it lasts

**Preview:** People want to support you. Help them.

There's a cultural moment happening.

"Support local." "Shop small." "Buy local."

People actively want to choose local businesses over chains. They feel good about it. They post about it. They tell their friends.

This is your moment.

But you have to be findable. You have to look alive. You have to make it easy for them to choose you.

Someone who wants to "support local" searches for your type of business. They're actively hoping to find a local option.

What do they find?

If your page looks active and authentic, they feel great about choosing you. It confirms they're making the right choice.

If your page looks abandoned, they hesitate. "Is this place even still good?"

The "support local" energy is real. People genuinely want to choose businesses like yours over faceless chains.

Don't make it hard for them.

Show up. Look alive. Be the local business they were hoping to find.

The chains can't compete with this. Nobody feels good about "supporting Starbucks." But they do feel good about supporting you.

Lean into it. You earned it by being local.
