**Subject:** 7 posts you could make in the next 10 minutes

**Preview:** It's simpler than you think.

The blank screen.

The blinking cursor.

The question that stops most business owners cold:

"What do I even post about?"

Here's the thing: you're probably overthinking this.

Local business social media isn't about being creative or clever or viral. It's about one thing:

Proving you're open.

That's it.

So here are 7 posts you could make right now, no creativity required:

1. **A photo of your space.** "Good morning from [Business Name]." Done.

2. **Your hours.** "Just a reminder - we're open until 7pm on Thursdays!" (People forget.)

3. **A product/service you offer.** "Did you know we also do [thing]?" (Your customers don't know everything you offer.)

4. **Behind the scenes.** Photo of you or your team actually working. "Tuesday morning prep."

5. **A customer win.** "Congrats to [customer] on [thing]!" (With permission, obviously.)

6. **A simple tip.** Share one piece of advice related to your industry.

7. **The weather.** Seriously. "Rainy day = perfect day for [your thing]." It's relatable.

None of these require:
- Professional photography
- Clever copywriting
- Trending audio
- Hours of planning

They just require posting.

([Glow Social](https://glowsocial.com) generates ideas like these for your specific business, by the way.)

The chains aren't posting groundbreaking content either. They're posting basic stuff. Regularly.

That's the whole secret. They just don't stop.

Pick one from the list. Post it today. See how it feels.
