# Week 30: You Know Things Nobody Else Knows

**Subject:** The stuff that's obvious to you

**Preview:** Your expertise is content gold.

You know things.

After years in your industry, you've accumulated knowledge that seems obvious to you. Just basic stuff. Common sense.

But it's not obvious to your customers.

The thing you say 10 times a day? They've never heard it.

The mistake you see everyone make? They don't know they're making it.

The tip that seems too simple to share? It would blow their minds.

Your expertise is content.

"3 signs your [thing] needs replacing"
"The question I get asked every day"
"What most people don't know about [your industry]"
"The mistake I see constantly"

This isn't hard to create. It's stuff you already know. Stuff you already say to customers.

You're just not posting it.

The chains can't do this. They have generic content from corporate. They don't have years of local expertise.

You do.

Every time a customer asks you something, that's a post idea. Every time you fix a problem someone didn't know they had, that's a post idea.

You're sitting on a goldmine of expertise. Use it.
