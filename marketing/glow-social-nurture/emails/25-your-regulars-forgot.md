# Week 25: Your Regulars Forgot About You

**Subject:** Out of sight, out of mind

**Preview:** Even loyal customers need reminders.

Your best customers love you.

They've been coming for years. They tell their friends. They're loyal.

But here's the uncomfortable truth:

They're also busy. And forgetful. And being marketed to by everyone else.

When you don't post, you disappear from their awareness.

Not intentionally. They still like you. They just... forgot. Life got busy. Something else grabbed their attention.

"Oh yeah, I haven't been to [Your Business] in a while. I should go back."

That thought? It doesn't happen automatically. It needs a trigger.

A post in their feed. A story that catches their eye. A reminder that you exist.

Without that trigger, weeks turn into months. Months turn into "I wonder if they're still open?"

Loyalty isn't permanent. It's maintained.

The chains understand this. They stay in your face. Constantly. Not because you don't know Starbucks exists. But because they want to be top-of-mind when you want coffee.

Your regulars love you. But love doesn't conquer forgetfulness.

A simple post saying "Happy Friday!" might be the trigger that brings someone back this weekend.
