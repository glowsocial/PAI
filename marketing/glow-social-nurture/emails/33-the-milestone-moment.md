# Week 33: You Missed the Milestone

**Subject:** The anniversary you didn't post about

**Preview:** Every milestone is content.

When's your business anniversary?

Did you post about it?

Most local business owners don't. The date comes and goes. Another year in business, unmarked.

But milestones are content. Easy content.

"5 years ago today, we opened our doors. Thank you for being part of our journey."

That's it. A photo of your storefront. Maybe a throwback if you have one.

People love milestone posts. They feel good. They're shareable. They make your community feel like part of your story.

Other milestones you're probably missing:

- Business anniversary
- Number of customers served ("Just helped our 1,000th customer!")
- Employee work anniversaries
- Expansion or renovation anniversaries
- Community involvement milestones ("10 years of sponsoring the little league team")

These aren't bragging. They're celebrating.

And they're incredibly easy to post. The content writes itself.

The chains do this religiously. "Starbucks turns 52!" It's free positive content.

Put your milestones in your calendar. Don't let them pass without posting.
