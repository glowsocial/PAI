# Week 23: The "I Don't Know What to Say" Problem

**Subject:** It's not about having photos

**Preview:** The real reason your page is quiet.

Most business owners think their social media problem is photos.

"I need better photos." "I don't have time to take photos." "My photos aren't good enough."

But that's not actually the problem.

The problem is knowing what to say.

You could have the best photos in the world. But if you sit there staring at the caption box with no idea what to write? Those photos stay in your camera roll forever.

That's why chains post so consistently. They have entire teams writing captions. The photos are almost secondary.

Words are harder than pictures.

Anyone can snap a photo of their workspace. But turning that into a post that sounds like you, that connects with your customers, that doesn't feel awkward or salesy? That's the hard part.

This is exactly why I built [Glow Social](https://glowsocial.com). It handles the "what do I say" problem so you can focus on running your business.

Because the photos aren't the bottleneck. The words are.
