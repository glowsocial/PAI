# Week 39: You Don't Need Motivation

**Subject:** Motivation is unreliable

**Preview:** Systems beat willpower every time.

You know what doesn't work?

Waiting until you feel motivated to post.

Motivation is fickle. It shows up sometimes. Disappears most of the time. Can't be scheduled or predicted.

You might feel motivated on a random Tuesday. You post three times. Feel great about yourself.

Then motivation vanishes for two months.

This is the cycle. Burst of energy, long silence, guilt, burst of energy, long silence.

Motivation-based posting doesn't work because motivation doesn't work.

What works is systems.

A system doesn't care if you're motivated. It just runs.

"I post every Tuesday and Thursday."
"I take a photo at the end of every job."
"I have content scheduled for the next month."

No motivation required. Just execution.

The chains don't rely on motivated employees. They have systems that run regardless of how anyone feels.

That's what you're competing against. Not creative people. Systems.

You need a system too. Something that runs even when you're tired. Busy. Not feeling it.

[Glow Social](https://glowsocial.com) is that system.
