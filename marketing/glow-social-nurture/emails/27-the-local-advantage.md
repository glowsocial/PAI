# Week 27: The One Thing Chains Can't Copy

**Subject:** You have something Starbucks doesn't

**Preview:** Use the advantage you're ignoring.

Starbucks has better systems than you.

Bigger budget. More people. Consistent posting. All of it.

But you have something they can never have:

You're local.

You're the owner. You live here. You coach little league. Your kids go to school with their kids.

You're not a corporation deployed from 2,000 miles away. You're a neighbor.

And that matters to people.

The chains can post generic content forever. They can't post "Thanks for 15 years in [Your Town]!" They can't share a photo of the owner at the community fundraiser. They can't say "See you at the game tonight!"

You can.

Local is your superpower. But only if you use it.

A generic local business with a dead social media page? Loses to the chains every time.

A local business that actually *feels* local online? That's something the chains can't compete with.

Show your face. Mention local stuff. Be a person, not a logo.

The chains will always beat you on consistency. Beat them on connection.

[Glow Social](https://glowsocial.com) helps with the consistency part. The local part is up to you.
