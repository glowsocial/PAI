# Week 16: The One Customer Math

**Subject:** Is one post worth one customer?

**Preview:** The math that changes everything.

Let me ask you a question:

If one social media post brought in one new customer, would it be worth it?

For most local businesses, one customer is worth somewhere between $50 and $500. Some businesses, it's thousands.

One post takes about 5 minutes if you're not overthinking it.

So the question becomes: is 5 minutes worth $50-500?

Obviously yes.

But here's the thing - you don't need every post to bring in a customer.

You just need your *presence* to not lose customers.

Every time someone checks your page and sees activity, that's trust. That's "oh good, they're still open." That's one less person who went to the chain instead.

You'll never know which post did it. You'll never get a customer who says "I came in because of your Tuesday post."

But you will notice, over time, that people find you. That referrals convert. That new faces show up.

And you'll never know which ones you lost because your page looked dead.

The math isn't "one post = one customer."

The math is "consistent posting = not losing customers you'll never know about."

The chains understand this math. That's why they never stop.
