# Week 35: It's Working Even When You Can't See It

**Subject:** The results you won't notice

**Preview:** Social media is a slow build.

You posted for a week. Nothing happened.

You posted for a month. Still nothing obvious.

So you stopped. "It doesn't work for my business."

Here's the thing about social media for local businesses:

The results are invisible.

You won't get a customer who says "I found you because of your Tuesday post."

You won't get a referral who says "I was going to go elsewhere but your Instagram looked active, so I came here instead."

You won't know how many people almost went to the chain but chose you because you looked alive online.

There's no tracking pixel for "they checked your page and felt good about coming in."

So you assume it's not working. Because you can't measure it.

But it is working. Slowly. Invisibly.

Every post is a tiny deposit in a trust account. You don't see the balance growing. You just notice, eventually, that people keep showing up.

The chains know this. They don't expect viral results from each post. They just know that showing up consistently, forever, compounds over time.

Don't quit because you can't see results. The results are there. You just can't measure them.
