
## You're not competing with who you think you are

It's not the other local businesses.

When I ask local business owners about their competition, they usually name the business down the street.

The other coffee shop. The other gym. The other salon.

But that's not who you're actually competing with.

Your real competition is the chain.

The Starbucks. The Planet Fitness. The Great Clips.

Not because they're better. They're usually not.

But because they've solved the visibility problem.

Think about what happens when someone new moves to town and searches for your type of business:

They see the chain. Posts from yesterday. Hundreds of reviews. Looks active, established, safe.

They see you. Maybe. If you show up at all. And if you do, your last post is from months ago.

The chain didn't win because they make better coffee or give better haircuts.

They won because they showed up. Consistently. Everywhere. All the time.

Here's the uncomfortable truth:

Your competition down the street? They're in the same boat as you.

Dead Facebook page. Abandoned Instagram. No recent posts.

You're both losing to the chains. Just in different ways.

The chains were deployed here with marketing budgets approved 2,000 miles away. You were too busy actually serving customers to post about it.

The local businesses that thrive aren't the ones beating other local businesses.

They're the ones who figured out how to look as active as the chains - without the corporate budget.

[Glow Social](https://glowsocial.com) is how you do that.

That's the real game.
