# Week 17: Your Website Isn't Enough

**Subject:** "But I have a website"

**Preview:** Different trust signals for different moments.

"I have a website. Isn't that enough?"

I hear this a lot. And I get the logic.

Your website has your hours, your services, your contact info. Everything someone needs to become a customer.

So why does social media matter?

Because websites and social media answer different questions.

**Your website answers:** "What do they do? How do I contact them?"

**Your social media answers:** "Are they still active? Are they legit? What's it actually like there?"

Different trust signals. Different moments in the decision.

Think about how you check out a new restaurant:

You might Google them and find their website. Okay, looks like a restaurant. Menu seems fine.

But then you check their Instagram. Are there recent photos? Does the food look good in real life? Are people actually there?

The website got you interested. The social media closed the deal.

Or killed it.

An active website with dead social media sends a weird signal. It's like a store with lights on but no one inside.

"Are they... open? This feels off."

The chains have both. Active website. Active social. Every trust signal covered.

Your website is doing its job. But it can't do social media's job.
