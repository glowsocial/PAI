# Week 31: Don't Just Post on Holidays

**Subject:** The holiday posting trap

**Preview:** Everyone posts on Christmas. Then what?

Here's a pattern I see constantly:

Business posts on Thanksgiving. "We're thankful for our customers!"

Then Christmas. "Happy holidays from our family to yours!"

Then New Year's. "Excited for 2025!"

Then... nothing until Valentine's Day.

Holiday posts are fine. But they're not a strategy.

They're the bare minimum. The "I should probably post something" posts.

And everyone does them. Your feed on Christmas is 47 local businesses all saying the same thing.

It's noise, not signal.

The real value isn't posting on holidays. It's posting *between* holidays.

That random Tuesday in February when nobody else is posting? That's when you stand out.

The chains understand this. They post on holidays AND every other day. The holidays don't replace the regular posting — they're in addition to it.

If your only posts are holidays, your page still looks mostly dead.

"Happy Easter!" followed by 3 months of silence still looks abandoned.

Post on holidays if you want. But don't let holidays be your entire strategy.
