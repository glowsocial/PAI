# Week 1: The Game Is Rigged

**Subject:** The game is rigged (and not in your favor)

**Preview:** Same customer. Same search bar. Different outcome.

There are two types of businesses in every town.

One type lives here. The owner coaches Little League. Sponsors the school fundraiser. Knows the regulars by name. Has been on the same corner for 15 years.

The other type was deployed here. Private equity bought a building. Corporate sent a manager. The marketing budget was approved in a conference room 2,000 miles away.

Guess which one looks more professional online?

The chain posts daily. Not because they care more - because they have people whose entire job is to post.

The local business posts whenever the owner has five free minutes. Which is never.

Same customer. Same search bar. Same split-second decision.

One looks alive. One looks abandoned.

**The customer picks the one that showed up.**

This is the game. And it's rigged against the people who are actually doing the work.

Not because you're bad at social media. Because you're too busy running the business to post about it.

That's why I built [Glow Social](https://glowsocial.com) - to help level the playing field.

Something to think about this week.
