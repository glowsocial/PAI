# Week 21: The Slow Season Trap

**Subject:** The worst time to stop posting

**Preview:** When you most need to post is when you least want to.

Business is slow.

It's natural to pull back. Cut costs. Wait it out.

And one of the first things to go? Social media.

"I'll pick it back up when things get busy again."

This is backwards.

Slow seasons are when you *most* need to be visible.

When things are busy, customers are coming in regardless. Your momentum carries you.

When things are slow, you're fighting for every customer. The ones who are looking need to find you.

But here's what happens:

Business slows down → You stop posting → You become less visible → Fewer people find you → Business slows down more

It's a death spiral disguised as cost-cutting.

Meanwhile, the chains never slow down. Starbucks doesn't stop posting in January because people are doing dry January. They post MORE.

They know that visibility during slow times is what creates busy times.

The businesses that thrive through slow seasons aren't the ones who go quiet.

They're the ones who stay visible while everyone else disappears.

Your competitors are pulling back right now. The chains aren't.

Which side do you want to be on?
