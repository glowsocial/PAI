# Week 40: A Year From Now

**Subject:** Where will your page be in 12 months?

**Preview:** The gap is going to grow either way.

A year from now, one of two things will be true:

**Scenario A:** You have a year's worth of posts. Your page looks thriving. Active. Alive. When someone searches for your type of business, you show up looking legitimate. Referrals land on a page that confirms what they heard. New residents find you and feel good about choosing you.

**Scenario B:** Your page looks the same as it does today. Or worse. The gap has grown. The last post is even older. When someone checks you out, they wonder if you're still open.

A year is going to pass either way.

The question is: which scenario do you want to be living in?

It doesn't take much to get to Scenario A. A few posts a week. Consistency over intensity. Showing up even when you don't feel like it.

The chains will definitely be in Scenario A. They never stop. They never question whether to post. The machine just runs.

You have a choice they don't have. You can decide.

But deciding to do nothing is still a decision. It's choosing Scenario B.

I don't want that for you. The whole point of these emails has been to help you see what's possible.

[Glow Social](https://glowsocial.com) can help you get to Scenario A. But ultimately, you have to choose.

What's it going to be?
