# Week 6: It's Not a Time Problem

**Subject:** "I don't have time for social media"

**Preview:** You're not wrong. But you're not right either.

"I don't have time for social media."

I hear this constantly. And I get it.

Running a business is all-consuming. Between customers, inventory, employees, bills, maintenance, and actually doing the work - who has time to think about Instagram?

But here's what I've realized:

It's not really a time problem. It's an energy problem.

Think about it.

Could you find 15 minutes three times a week? Technically, yes.

But those 15 minutes require you to:
- Think of something to post
- Find or take a photo
- Write something clever (or at least coherent)
- Actually open the app and do it

That's not 15 minutes of time. That's 15 minutes of *creative energy* when you're already running on fumes.

After a long day of putting out fires, the last thing you want to do is be creative on demand.

So you don't post. Not because you don't have 15 minutes.

Because you don't have 15 minutes of *creative energy* left.

And then the guilt kicks in. Which makes it worse.

Meanwhile, Starbucks doesn't have this problem. They have people whose only job is social media. They're not running on fumes at 8pm trying to think of something clever to post.

That's the unfair part. You're competing against people who have the one thing you don't: dedicated energy for this.

The game is rigged. It's not your fault you're losing.
