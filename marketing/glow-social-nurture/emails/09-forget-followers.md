Quick question:

How many followers does your dentist have on Instagram?

No idea, right?

How about your mechanic? Your accountant? The restaurant you went to last week?

You don't know. Because you never checked. Because it doesn't matter.

Here's what actually matters for local businesses:

**Do you look active and open?**

That's it. That's the whole game.

Not followers. Not likes. Not engagement rate. Not viral moments.

Just: when someone lands on your page, do they see recent activity? Or do they see a ghost town?

And more importantly: your page looks alive.

The vanity metrics are for influencers.
