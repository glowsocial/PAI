# Week 29: Show Your Work

**Subject:** The easiest content you're not making

**Preview:** Before and after. That's it.

Here's the lowest-effort, highest-impact content for almost any business:

Before and after.

Haircut? Before and after.
Landscaping? Before and after.
Car detailing? Before and after.
Home renovation? Before and after.
Teeth whitening? Before and after.

It works because it's proof. Undeniable proof that you do what you say you do.

No clever caption needed. No trending audio. Just two photos.

"Before → After. Another happy customer."

Done.

And yet most local businesses never post these.

Why? Because it requires remembering to take the "before" photo when you're busy trying to start the actual work.

I get it. You're focused on the job, not the content.

But if you can train yourself to grab a quick "before" photo — just one photo before you start — you have content for later.

The chains don't have this advantage, actually. A before/after at Starbucks is... what? Before coffee, after coffee?

Your work creates transformation. Visible transformation. That's powerful content just sitting there.

Start grabbing "before" photos. Your future self will thank you.
