# Week 32: Your Team Is Content

**Subject:** Meet [Employee Name]

**Preview:** The content walking around your business.

You have content walking around your business every day.

Your team.

"Meet Sarah, she's been with us for 3 years."
"Shoutout to Mike for going above and beyond today."
"Our team getting ready for a busy weekend."

This content works because it humanizes your business.

It shows real people. Not a logo. Not a faceless company. Actual humans.

Customers love this. They like knowing who they'll interact with. They like seeing the faces behind the business.

And your employees usually love it too. A little recognition goes a long way.

The chains try to do this, but it feels corporate. Forced. "Meet Store #4792's employee of the month!"

You can make it real. Personal. Warm.

"Sarah's been doing hair for 12 years, and her specialty is color corrections. Book with her if you've had a salon disaster!"

That's not content the chains can create. It's too real.

Your team isn't just your staff. They're content. Story opportunities. Proof that real people work at your business.

Use them.
