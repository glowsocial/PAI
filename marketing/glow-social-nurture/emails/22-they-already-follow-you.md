# Week 22: They Already Follow You

**Subject:** You have an audience. You're ignoring them.

**Preview:** Those followers aren't random.

Check your follower count real quick.

200? 500? Maybe 1,000?

Those aren't random people. Those are people who actively chose to see your content.

They walked into your business, pulled out their phone, searched for you, and tapped "Follow."

That's remarkable when you think about it.

They wanted to hear from you. They were interested enough to take action.

And now? Crickets.

Your last post was... when? They haven't heard from you in weeks. Months, maybe.

Those followers didn't unfollow. They're still there. Still technically listening.

But you're not saying anything.

It's like having an email list and never sending emails. A phone number you never call.

You built an audience. A small one, sure. But a real one.

Now use it.

You don't need to go viral to reach new people. You have people who already raised their hand.

The chains don't ignore their followers. They feed them content constantly.

Your followers chose you. Show up for them.
