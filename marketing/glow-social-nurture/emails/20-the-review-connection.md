# Week 20: Reviews and Social Media Work Together

**Subject:** Your reviews are lonely

**Preview:** They need backup.

You might have great reviews.

5 stars on Google. Glowing testimonials. Customers who rave about you.

That's amazing. Seriously.

But reviews alone aren't enough anymore.

Here's why:

When someone finds your reviews, they almost always check your social media next. It's just how people verify businesses now.

Great reviews + active social = "Okay, this place is legit."

Great reviews + dead social = "Hmm, these reviews are from 6 months ago. Are they still good?"

Your reviews are doing their job. But they're working alone.

Social media is the backup. The confirmation. The "yes, they're still active and still great."

Think about it from the customer's perspective:

They see your reviews. They're interested. They click over to your Instagram. And they see... nothing recent.

Now they're second-guessing. "Are those reviews still accurate? Have things changed? Is something wrong?"

The reviews got them to the door. The dead social media made them hesitate.

The chains understand this. Their reviews AND their social media are both active. Every trust signal reinforcing the others.

Your reviews are doing heavy lifting. Give them some help.
