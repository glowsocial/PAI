# Week 26: Nobody Remembers Your Old Posts

**Subject:** You can post that again

**Preview:** Your audience has goldfish memory.

Here's a secret: you can repost old content.

Seriously.

That post from 6 months ago that got decent engagement? Post it again.

That photo you really liked? Use it again.

That tip that resonated? Share it again.

Nobody remembers. Nobody's keeping track.

We have this weird fear that people will notice. "Wait, didn't they post this before?"

They won't. They've scrolled past thousands of posts since then. They've forgotten.

And even if someone did notice? So what? It was good content worth sharing again.

The chains do this constantly. They have rotating content. The same promotions. The same seasonal posts. The same basic "good morning" energy.

Because they know it works. And they know nobody's tracking.

You don't need infinite original ideas. You need a handful of good posts and the willingness to reuse them.

Your greatest hits can play on repeat.

This takes so much pressure off. You don't need to be endlessly creative. You just need to have posted enough to have a back catalog.

Start now. Post something. In 6 months, you can post it again.
