# Video 3: Glow Social Complete Walkthrough

## Recording Notes

- Record the entire signup → onboarding → post generation → approval flow

- Move slowly — this audience needs to see every click
- Pause on each screen for 2-3 seconds before narrating so viewers can read

## THE SCRIPT

### [SCREEN: glowsocial.com homepage]

**[0:00 – 0:30] HOOK**

> "I'm going to show you exactly how Glow Social works. Every step. From the moment you land on the website to the moment your first social media post goes live. No skipping ahead, no shortcuts. If you've been thinking about signing up but you want to see what you're getting into first — this is the video."

---

### [SCREEN: Click the signup / get started button]

**[0:30 – 2:00] STEP 1: SIGNING UP**

> "So here we are at glowsocial.com. You click 'Get Started' — and the first thing it asks for is your email and a password. Pretty standard stuff.
>
> Once you're in, you'll see the pricing. It's forty-nine dollars a month. No annual contract. No hidden fees. You can cancel anytime. If you choose to pay annually, you save a bit, but monthly is totally fine.
>
> Go ahead and pick your plan, enter your payment info, and you're in. The whole signup takes about two minutes.
>
> And I want to set expectations here: once you're signed up, there's about five minutes of questions about your business, and then you're done. That's all the work you'll ever do in this tool. Five minutes, one time."

---

### [SCREEN: The onboarding questions / business setup]

**[2:00 – 4:30] STEP 2: TELLING US ABOUT YOUR BUSINESS**

> "Okay, so now you're inside Glow Social, and the first thing it wants to know is about your business.
>
> It's going to ask you a few things:
>
> First — what industry are you in? And you'll see a list here. Roofing, dental, plumbing, real estate, auto repair, restaurants, landscaping — there's a bunch of categories. Pick the one that fits.
>
> Next — your business name. And your location, if you serve a specific area. So if you're 'Mike's Plumbing in Scottsdale,' that goes right here.
>
> Then it asks what makes your business different. And don't overthink this. It's not a college essay. Just something like 'We've been family-owned for twenty years' or 'We offer same-day service' or 'We specialize in commercial roofing.' One or two sentences is plenty.
>
> Why does this matter? Because these answers are what makes your posts sound like YOUR business, not just generic 'Happy Monday' content. The more specific you are here, the better your posts turn out.
>
> You can always come back and change these answers later, so don't stress about getting it perfect right now."

---

### [SCREEN: Social media account connection flow]

**[4:30 – 6:30] STEP 3: CONNECTING YOUR SOCIAL MEDIA**

> "Now it's time to connect your social media accounts. This is the part where Glow Social gets permission to post on your behalf.
>
> You'll see options here for Facebook, Instagram, and Google Business Profile. Let me walk through each one.
>
> For Facebook — you click 'Connect Facebook,' it opens a Facebook login window, you log in with your regular Facebook account, and then it asks you to select your business page. Important: make sure you select your business PAGE, not your personal profile. Your personal profile is you. Your page is your business. If you're not sure which is which, your page is the one with your business name and logo on it.
>
> For Instagram — it works through Facebook, actually. If your Instagram is connected to your Facebook business page, it'll show up automatically. If it's not connected yet, there's a quick step to link them in Meta's settings. We have a help article for this if you get stuck.
>
> For Google Business Profile — you click 'Connect Google,' sign in with the Google account that manages your business profile, and select your business. If you've ever updated your hours or responded to a review on Google, it's that same account.
>
> Now, if any of this feels confusing — that's okay. A lot of our customers need a hand with this part. You can reach out to support and we'll walk you through it. This is the most technical part of the whole process, and once it's done, you never have to do it again."

---

### [SCREEN: Dashboard showing generated post ideas / sparks]

**[6:30 – 9:00] STEP 4: YOUR POSTS GET CREATED**

> "Alright, this is the fun part. Your business info is in, your accounts are connected, and now Glow Social creates your posts.
>
> What you're looking at here is your content. These are post ideas that were generated specifically for your type of business.
>
> Let me click into a few so you can see what they actually look like.
>
> [Click into first post] Here's one. It says: [read the post content out loud]. See how that's actually relevant? It's not some random motivational quote. It's a real post about what your business does.
>
> [Click into second post] Here's another one: [read it]. This one is more of a behind-the-scenes style post. It makes your business feel real and human, not like a corporation.
>
> [Click into third post] And here's a third: [read it]. This is what we call an authority post — it shows that you know your stuff. Your customers read this and think 'okay, these people are legit.'
>
> So you'll get a mix like this. Some posts are about your services, some are behind-the-scenes, some establish you as an expert. It's a healthy mix that keeps your social media looking active and interesting.
>
> And these are custom for your industry. A roofer gets roofing posts. A dentist gets dental posts. You're not getting the same content as everyone else."

---

### [SCREEN: Calendar view or post review/approval screen]

**[9:00 – 11:00] STEP 5: REVIEWING AND APPROVING**

> "Now, before anything goes live, you get to see everything first. Nothing gets posted without your approval.
>
> Here's the calendar view — you can see all your upcoming posts laid out by date. Each one is scheduled for a specific day. They're spread out through the month so your social media looks consistently active, not like you dumped everything at once.
>
> If you want to edit a post, you click on it, and you can change the text. Maybe you want to swap out a detail, or add something specific that happened that week. You can do that.
>
> But here's what most of our customers do: they look through the posts, think 'yeah, those look good,' and hit approve. Done. The whole review might take three minutes.
>
> And honestly? That's fine. The posts are already written for your industry. They're already professional. You don't HAVE to customize them. If you want to, great. If you'd rather just approve and move on, that works too.
>
> That's kind of the whole point — you spent five minutes setting up your business info, three minutes reviewing your posts, and now your social media is handled for the entire month."

---

### [SCREEN: Show a post appearing on a Facebook page or Google Business Profile]

**[11:00 – 12:30] STEP 6: YOUR POSTS GO LIVE**

> "And now they go out. Automatically. On the schedule.
>
> Here's what it looks like on the other end — this is a Facebook page with Glow Social posts going out. See the dates? Consistent. Spread throughout the month. The page looks active. It looks alive. Anyone who lands on this page thinks 'okay, this business is real and they're paying attention.'
>
> Same thing on Google Business Profile — posts show up in Google search results, which means when someone Googles your type of business, they see your recent updates right there in the listing.
>
> And the best part? This just keeps happening. Every month, new posts are created. Every month, you spend three minutes reviewing them. Every month, they go out on schedule. You never have to write a caption, design a graphic, or remember to post on a Tuesday. It's handled."

---

### [SCREEN: Pricing page]

**[12:30 – 13:30] WHAT IT COSTS (RECAP)**

> "Let me just recap what you're getting here, because I want to make sure it's clear.
>
> Forty-nine dollars a month. That gets you:
>
> Twelve posts per month, written for your specific industry.
> Posts go to Facebook, Instagram, and Google Business Profile.
> You review and approve everything before it goes out.
> It's all automatic after that.
> No contract. Cancel anytime.
>
> Compare that to hiring someone: a freelancer is five hundred to a thousand a month. An agency is one to two thousand. And with both of those, you still have to do calls, give feedback, approve things in long email chains.
>
> With Glow Social, you sign up, spend five minutes on setup, and three minutes a month reviewing posts. That's it."

### [SCREEN: glowsocial.com with signup button]

> "So that's the whole thing. Signup to first post — that's the entire process.
>
> If your social media has been sitting there dead and you've been meaning to do something about it, this is the something.
>
> Go to glowsocial.com. Pick your plan. Five minutes of setup. And you'll never think about social media again.
>
> Link's in the description."
