# Glow Social YouTube Content Plan

## The Problem This Solves

A boomer looked at the YouTube channel and had **no idea** what Glow Social does. That means:
- The current content assumes too much knowledge
- There's no "explain it like I'm a local business owner who's never used AI" content
- People who NEED this product the most can't figure out what it is from watching

**This audience doesn't want hype. They want clarity, proof, and trust.**

---

## Content Principles for This Audience

1. **Show, don't tell.** Screen recordings > talking head monologues
2. **Go slow.** What feels painfully slow to you is "finally, someone who makes sense" to them
3. **Use their language.** Not "AI-powered content generation" — say "we write your social media posts for you"
4. **Address the real fear.** They're not afraid of the price. They're afraid of looking stupid online.
5. **Name the thing.** Don't say "our platform." Say "Glow Social." Every time.
6. **Repeat the price.** $49/month. Say it early. Say it often. Removes the "what's the catch" anxiety.
7. **No jargon.** No "hooks," no "content pillars," no "engagement metrics." Say "posts," "ideas," "likes and comments."

## Production Framework

### Equipment Needed (Minimal)
- Screen recording: Loom (free) or OBS
- Talking head: iPhone on a tripod, ring light, quiet room
- Editing: CapCut (free) or Descript
- Thumbnails: Canva

### Video Structure Template
Every video follows this structure:

```
[0:00] HOOK — State the problem in their words
[0:30] PROBLEM — Why this matters (don't assume they know)
[2:00] SOLUTION — What Glow Social does about it
[4:00] PROOF — Screen recording, examples, or customer story
[7:00] HOW — Show them exactly what to do next
[8:00] CTA — Go to glowsocial.com
```

### Thumbnail Rules
- Show the DASHBOARD or a real social media post (not your face, unless you're well-known)
- Use big, readable text (5 words max on thumbnail)
- Before/after style works extremely well for this audience
- Red arrows and circles are cliché but they WORK on boomers

### Title Rules
- Lead with the OUTCOME, not the product name
- Use words they'd actually Google
- Examples:
  - ✅ "Social Media for Your Business — Without Doing It Yourself"
  - ✅ "Why Your Business Looks Closed Online"
  - ❌ "Glow Social Platform Overview Q1 2026"
  - ❌ "How AI Content Generation Changes Local SEO"
