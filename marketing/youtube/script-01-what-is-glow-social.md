# Video 1: What Is Glow Social?

## Recording Notes

- Open the Glow Social dashboard before you start recording
- Have a sample client account ready to show (or your own demo account)
- Have a browser tab open to a Google Business Profile search result (search "plumber near me" or similar)
- Have a browser tab open to glowsocial.com
- Speak like you're explaining this to the guy on the phone today
- If you stumble, keep going — this isn't a TED talk, it's a product demo
- **IMPORTANT:** Do NOT do the full pricing comparison in this video — that's Video 6. Just state the price cleanly and move on.

## THE SCRIPT

### [SCREEN: Google search results for "plumber near me" or similar local search]

**[0:00 – 0:40] HOOK**

> "If you run a local business — you're a roofer, a dentist, a plumber, a chiropractor, whatever it is — and you know your social media is basically dead right now...
>
> Maybe your last Facebook post was four months ago. Maybe your Google Business Profile hasn't been updated since you set it up. Maybe you've thought about posting but every time you sit down to do it, you stare at the screen for five minutes and then go back to doing actual work.
>
> This video is for you. I'm going to show you exactly what Glow Social is, what it costs, and how it works. The whole thing. No fluff."

---

### [SCREEN: Show a Google search result where one business has recent posts and one doesn't]

**[0:40 – 2:30] THE PROBLEM**

> "Here's the thing nobody tells you when you start a business: your social media is basically your storefront online.
>
> When someone searches 'plumber near me' or 'dentist in Phoenix' or whatever your business is — Google shows them a list. And the businesses that have recent activity, recent posts, recent reviews — they show up higher. They look alive.
>
> The businesses with nothing? They look closed. And I don't mean that figuratively. Actual potential customers will see your Facebook page, see that your last post was from last summer, and think 'Are they still in business?'
>
> And here's the part that really hurts: those customers don't call you to ask. They just call someone else. You never even know you lost them."

### [SCREEN: Show a dead-looking Facebook business page — last post months ago]

> "Now, you already know this. You're not watching this video because you think social media doesn't matter. You're watching because you know it matters and you just don't have the time. Or honestly, you don't want to do it.
>
> And I get it. You didn't become a roofer so you could write Facebook posts. You didn't open a dental practice so you could come up with Instagram captions. That's not your job.
>
> But somebody has to do it. And you've probably looked into it — agencies, freelancers, posting tools — and it's either confusing, expensive, or both.
>
> So that's the problem: you need social media to look active, but you don't have the time or the energy to figure it out. That's exactly why Glow Social exists."

---

### [SCREEN: Navigate to glowsocial.com — show the signup flow]

**[2:30 – 6:00] HOW GLOW SOCIAL WORKS**

> "So let me show you what Glow Social actually does. I'm going to walk through the whole thing."

### [SCREEN: Show the signup/onboarding flow]

> "You go to glowsocial.com and sign up. The first thing we ask is some basic information about your business. What's your email address? What's your name? What do you do? You don't even have to have a website to get started.
>
> Signing up takes about two minutes. And I want to be clear — this is the only homework you will ever do. After this, you're done. We take it from here."

### [SCREEN: Show the account connection step]

> "Next, you connect your social media accounts. That means Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile — whatever platforms you use.
>
> If you're not sure how to do this part, that's okay. We can help you. You're not on your own. But for most people, it's just logging in and clicking 'allow.'"

### [SCREEN: Show the dashboard after connecting accounts]

> "After that, you can just sit back. Once your business info is in and your accounts are connected, Glow Social gets to work."

### [SCREEN: Show topics/sparks being generated — walk through the flow]

> "We come up with what we think you should talk about. When you approve that, we come up with what we call 'sparks' — ideas for social media posts. Once you're cool with those, we turn those into posts with images and captions that work across all of social media.
>
> These aren't generic 'Happy Monday!' posts. They're specific to your industry and relevant to what your customers actually care about. They make your business look active, look professional, and look like you're paying attention."

### [SCREEN: Show posts going out on social media]

> "Once the posts are ready, they go out automatically. They get posted to your Facebook, your Instagram, your Google Business Profile — on a schedule, spread throughout the month, so it looks natural.
>
> And then next month? Same thing. New topics, new sparks, new posts. Twelve posts a month, forty-nine dollars, and you never think about social media again."

---

### [SCREEN: Show the Glow Social homepage or dashboard]

**[6:00 – 7:30] WHO THIS IS FOR**

> "So who is this actually for?
>
> It's for the roofer who's great at roofing but hasn't posted on Facebook since last spring.
>
> It's for the dentist whose receptionist used to handle social media but stopped because she got busy with, you know, actual patients.
>
> It's for the plumber who knows his competitors are posting every day but can't figure out how they find the time.
>
> It's for the auto repair shop, the chiropractor, the landscaper, the real estate agent — anyone running a real business who just needs their social media to not look abandoned.
>
> You don't need to go viral. You don't need to become an influencer. You don't need to do trending dances. You just need twelve posts a month that make you look open, active, and professional. That's what Glow Social does."

### [SCREEN: Show glowsocial.com with the signup button visible]

**[7:30 – 8:30] THE CLOSE**

> "If any of that sounded like you — if you've been meaning to fix your social media for months and you just haven't gotten to it — go to glowsocial.com.
>
> It's forty-nine dollars a month, no contract, cancel anytime. You'll answer a few questions about your business, connect your accounts, and within a few days you'll have posts going out on your social media.
>
> That's it. No calls to schedule. No strategy sessions. No homework. You sign up, we handle it, your social media looks alive again.
