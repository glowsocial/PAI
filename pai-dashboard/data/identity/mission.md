# Mission

## The Problem I'm Solving

Local businesses need organic social media to look open online. Nobody gets into business thinking "I want to be active on social media"—but the reality is brutal: if you don't have a social presence, you don't show up in Google searches.

And it's not enough to just *have* a profile. You have to keep publishing.

Most local business owners don't know that Google Business Profile is a social network that should be updated regularly. They don't realize that consistent posting gives them a better shot at showing up in "blank near me" searches. They just want to do the work they're good at—roofing, dentistry, plumbing—not become content creators.

Meanwhile, millennials and younger won't call a business that looks abandoned online. If your last post was 6 months ago, they assume you're closed.

## My Approach

Make social media invisible. Customers sign up, and then they never think about social media again. It just works.

- 12 posts per month, automatically generated and scheduled
- Industry-specific content that sounds human
- Google Business Profile updates that actually matter
- No calls, no meetings, no "strategy sessions"—just results

The magic is in the 96 hooks per client: customized angles that keep content fresh without repeating. Built once, runs forever.

## The Vision

**1,000 customers.** Happy ones. With churn low enough to stay around 1,000.

That's ~$50K/month. More than enough.

But the money isn't really the point. The point is:

**Solid processes that don't require 40 hours at a desk.**

So I can be an involved parent. Go on trips for spring break, summer break, fall break. Cement good relationships with my kids while they still like me.

Customers, not clients. No high-touch, high-ticket drama. People sign up, it works, they don't worry about it. Everyone wins.

## Current Focus

Building Glow Social to the point where growth is the only problem left to solve. Product works. Operations are smooth. Now it's just about getting to 1,000.
