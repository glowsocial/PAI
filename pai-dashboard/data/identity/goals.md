# Goals

## Active Goals

### Goal 1: 100 Glow Social Customers
- **Target:** 100 paying customers with low churn
- **Current:** ~17 customers (not all paid)
- **Timeline:** End of 2026
- **Revenue at goal:** ~$5,000/month
- **Status:** In progress

**The real blocker:** It's not leads, conversion, or churn. It's **product confidence**.

Glow Social is *so close* to feeling done. Onboarding works. The system runs. But bugs keep surfacing, and every new Stripe notification triggers anxiety instead of excitement. 

The path forward isn't "more marketing"—it's getting to the point where new customers feel like good news, not stress. That means:
- Squashing the remaining bugs
- Building trust in the system
- Shifting from "builder mode" to "growth mode"

**Next action:** Keep stabilizing. Get to the point where a new customer feels like celebration, not concern.
