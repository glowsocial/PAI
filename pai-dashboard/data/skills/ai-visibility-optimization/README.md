## Key Concepts

### AI Visibility Score (0-110)
- 0-30: Critical - Not visible
- 31-50: Low - Minimal presence
- 51-70: Moderate - Emerging
- 71-85: Good - Strong presence
- 86-110: Excellent - Dominant

### The 5% of AEO
Most AEO work is wasted. Focus on the 5% that drives impact:
1. **New Pages** - Create landing pages for AEO topics you haven't covered
2. **Content Enhancement** - Fill gaps on existing pages, answer questions better
3. **Citation Optimization** - Get mentioned by the URLs that LLMs cite most

Avoid: Technical AEO (page speed, crawl analysis) - drives little to no impact.

### The 3 Core Pages First
Before creating 15-20 topic pages, establish brand authority with 3 foundation pages:

1. **Context Page (About)** - Brand definition, founding story, what you do
   - Schema: Organization, Founder, SoftwareApplication
   - Validation: "Explain what [Brand] does and who founded it"

2. **Proof Page (Case Studies)** - Evidence with quantifiable metrics
   - Schema: CaseStudy, Review
   - Validation: "Show me case studies or proof that [Brand] works"

3. **Insight Page (Blog/Resources)** - 10+ first-principle insights with outbound links
   - Schema: FAQPage, HowTo
   - Validation: "What does [Brand/Founder] say about [topic]?"

**Internal Linking Loop:** About → Case Studies → Insights → About

**Why it works:** LLMs don't crawl like Google. They cross-reference authority. These 3 pages train the machine on WHO you are and WHY you're authoritative.

### AEO Topics (Not Individual Questions)
An AEO Topic is a cluster of hundreds of questions with similar intent:
- **Wrong approach**: 90 separate pages for 90 questions
- **Right approach**: 1 page that targets 100+ related questions

Example AEO Topic: "social media content generation"
- Answers 200+ questions: "how to create social content faster", "automated social media posts", "AI content generation for social", "best social media content tools", etc.

### Product Questions
Not all questions show products in answers. Focus on **Product Questions**:
- ✅ "what's the best social media tool for small business" (shows products)
- ❌ "definition of social media marketing" (no products shown)

Travel, commerce, local, tech questions show products most often.
News and food show products less than half the time.

### Content Strategy
- Create topic cluster pages (not individual Q&A posts)
- Each page targets 100-500 related questions
- Focus on Product Questions only
- Add Article schema on every page

### Citation Strategy
LLMs use RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation):
1. Search runs for your question
2. LLM summarizes the top search results (citations)
3. The more citations mention you, the more likely you appear

**Optimization**: Get mentioned by the specific URLs that are cited most.

### Share of Answers (Not Position)
Track how often you appear across:
- Multiple surfaces (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini)
- Multiple question variations (100+ per topic)
- Multiple runs (same question can yield different answers)

Example: 35% Share of Answers = you appear in 35 out of 100 question/surface/run combinations.
