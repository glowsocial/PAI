# THE 7-STEP FORMULA

1. **BENEFIT** - Lead with your strongest, most profound value proposition
2. **EXPANSION** - Future-pace the benefit, showing its impact on their life
3. **POSITIVE** - Explain exactly what they'll get (the offer)
4. **PROOF** - Validate claims with evidence, statistics, testimonials
5. **NEGATIVE** - Show consequences of inaction (pain of missing out)
6. **SUMMARY** - Revisit all benefits and impact
7. **ACTION** - Clear, specific call-to-action with guarantee

# PSYCHOLOGICAL PRINCIPLES

## The Power of Questions (Step 1)
- Questions create open loops in the brain
- "Will there be..." forces engagement
- Address fundamental concerns

## Future-Pacing (Step 2)
- "The next few years will..."
- "Those who prepare now will..."
- Creates mental ownership before purchase

## Specificity Sells (Step 3)
- Exact numbers ($16, 26 issues, 24% savings)
- Clear deliverables
- Defined timeframes

## Credibility Cascade (Step 4)
- Historical accuracy builds trust
- Specific dates and predictions
- Name real examples

## Pain > Pleasure (Step 5)
- We sprint from pain, jog to pleasure
- "Dangers" and "blunders" motivate
- Fear of missing out drives action

## Completeness (Step 6)
- Comprehensive benefits reduce objections
- "Anything that affects you..."
- No stone left unturned

## Friction Removal (Step 7)
- Simple, clear instructions
- Money-back guarantee
- "Do it with this guarantee..."
